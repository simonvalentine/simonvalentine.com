What this site collects

This is a simple information website. It has no forms, accounts or shop, and it does not set its own cookies or use analytics or advertising trackers.

The site is hosted by GitHub Pages. Like most web hosts, GitHub may record technical information such as your IP address and browser type in server logs to keep the service secure and running. You can read GitHub’s privacy statement.

Fonts are loaded from Google Fonts, which receives your IP address when the fonts are downloaded. See Google’s privacy policy.

If you email me or message me on LinkedIn, I use what you send only to reply and to follow up on your enquiry. I do not sell or share it.

This site links to LinkedIn, SoundCloud and my ventures’ websites. Those sites have their own privacy policies.

Your rights
