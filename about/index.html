A New Zealander in hiring, by way of the world.

I have spent more than twenty years in Talent Acquisition, working across cultures and continents. Here is how it started.

  1. Growing up

    I grew up in rural New Zealand, one of seven children of two hard-working parents. We lived in a number of small towns, my parents often worked two jobs, and a lot of my life lessons came from my brothers and sisters.

  2. Into recruitment

    When I finished my degree I asked my professor how I could apply what I had learnt. He said the best place was a company’s Human Resources department, and the best way in was through recruitment. I started straight out of university in New Zealand.

  3. Talent Acquisition, worldwide

    Twenty-plus years later I am still in Talent Acquisition. The work has taken me around the world and exposed me to many cultures, and today I split my time between Switzerland, England and New Zealand.
